Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who we are

zachadamseffect.com is the home page of the Zach Adams Effect YouTube channel, operated by Zach Vorhies. This policy explains what is collected on this website and what the tools behind the channel do with data.

This website collects nothing about you

  • There are no accounts, forms, sign-ups or newsletters here, so we never ask you for personal information.
  • These pages set no cookies and run no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts.
  • The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record ordinary server logs (such as IP address and browser user agent) for security and operating the service, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Following the links to YouTube takes you to Google's properties, where YouTube's and Google's own policies apply, not this one.

The video publishing tool

Videos are published to the channel by a private application ("Zach Adams Effect Uploader", Google Cloud project zachadamseffectapp, OAuth client hermes-uploader) that uses YouTube API Services. In connection with that tool:

  • It requests a single permission, youtube.upload, and uses it only to upload video files to the channel the owner controls.
  • It acts only on the account that authorizes it. It does not read or collect information about any other YouTube user or channel, and it never connects to a visitor's Google Account.
  • Its access credentials are stored on the owner's own machine, are never shared, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Information obtained through the Google APIs is not sold, transferred or disclosed to third parties, is not used for advertising or to determine creditworthiness, and is not used to train machine learning models.
  • Zach Vorhies's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

This site and the channel are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the date at the top of this page will be updated and the new version posted at this same URL.
