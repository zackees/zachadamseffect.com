You can, and it'd cost $400 — but you'd get two hours of my time and none of the rest. No second machine, no skill packs, no routing table, no month of support. Most people who try that come back for the Deep Dive.

Which one should I pick?

Never done this before? The Bootcamp. Done it and it's sitting unused? The Deep Dive — that's the exact problem it solves. Running a business, or more than one machine? The Full Stack.

I'm not technical. Is this for me?

If you can install software, yes. I do the work on your machine while you watch and I explain everything in plain language. One customer came in having spent months on ChatGPT projects that still weren't working; she left with four self-learning agents she can drive from her phone.

Will this work on my computer?

Depends on your hardware, and hardware is genuinely the hard part. One tip from a customer that makes a real difference: have your accounts set up beforehand, make sure your system is updated, and check your RAM against the manufacturer's spec. Do that and you'll get far more out of the session.

Do you do this for businesses?

Yes — the Full Stack covers a team of three. For a full business deployment, that's a separate scoped engagement starting with a two-hour scoping session. Book a Deep Dive and tell me what you need; I'll tell you honestly if it's worth building.
